# Community Conduct

Lee Relay aims to be a technically rigorous and welcoming open-source project.

Be respectful, specific, and evidence-driven. Critique code and ideas rather than people. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or expose private information about other contributors or users.

Security reports and bug reports often involve private browser data. Never pressure someone to publish sensitive logs or screenshots publicly.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions that are abusive, spammy, unsafe, intentionally misleading, or disclose private/security-sensitive information.
